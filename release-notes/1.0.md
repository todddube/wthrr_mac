## What's in 1.0

### Weather Effects
- **Snow** — procedural snowflakes with three crystal shapes (dots, star plates, dendrites) assigned by size; per-flake rotation, wobble, and blue tint; drift accumulation with adjustable depth and melt rate; Screen Frost ice crystal formations creep from edges during heavy snowfall
- **Rain** — wind-angled streaks, expanding double-ring impact splashes, persistent puddles with specular shine and ripple rings
- **Thunderstorm** — heavy rain with dark storm clouds, forced lightning strikes, all intensity knobs at maximum
- **Fog** — slow-drifting translucent gradient bands and large soft wisp particles
- **Aurora Borealis** — rippling vertical bands of green, purple, and teal; animated silhouette birds with independent speeds, sizes, and flap phases; configurable count, size, and wildness
- **AI Storm** — neon glitch particles, scan line distortion, circuit-trace data bolts, corruption patches, alien ships with organic steering, screen fracture cracks; 4 color palettes: Cyberpunk, Synthwave, Matrix, Inferno

### Live Weather
Auto-pulls real conditions from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) — applies weather type and intensity from your actual location. Shows location name and temperature in the header.

### Presets
9 built-in presets plus save/load/delete of custom named presets. Import and export as JSON to share between machines.

### Settings
- **Appearance** — particle amount, speed, opacity, per-weather color controls, snow melt rate, AI Storm density, aurora bird count/size/wildness
- **Environment** — wind strength/direction/angle, cloud size/speed/opacity, drift accumulation depth, lightning frequency
- **Presets** — built-in + user presets, save, delete, import/export JSON
- **Behavior** — Launch at Login, Seasonal auto-switch, Night Dimming, Low Power Mode

### Settings Weather Picker
Persistent weather type picker in the settings window — switch modes without leaving any tab; controls update instantly.

### Multi-Display
Overlays span all connected displays or target specific monitors. Windows rebuild automatically on display changes.

### Global Hotkey
Pause/Resume with a configurable global hotkey (default Cmd+Shift+P), works when the app isn't focused.

### Screenshot Preview
Right-click the menu bar icon → Screenshot Preview for a clean 1280×800 capture window.
