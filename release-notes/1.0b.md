# wthrr 1.0b — What's New

## Weather Modes
- **Aurora Borealis** — rippling color bands across all your displays
- **Thunderstorm** — dark clouds, heavy rain, and forced lightning
- **Fog** — slow drifting translucent bands and soft wisp particles
- **AI Storm** — alien ships, screen fractures, data bolts, and 4 neon palettes

## Live Data
- **Live Weather** — pulls real conditions from Open-Meteo and auto-applies effect + intensity

## Controls & UI
- **Live 64px preview strip** — see every change in real time as you tune
- **Environment section** — Wind and Atmosphere controls unified in one place
- **Reroll button** — instantly randomize a fresh look from the header
- **Intensity quick-taps** — Light, Medium, Heavy set everything at once

## Presets & Customization
- **9 built-in presets** — Blizzard, Monsoon, Aurora, Glitch Overload, and more
- **Preset import/export** — share your custom setups as JSON
- **Color theming** — hue slider for Snow, Rain, and Fog; palette swap for AI Storm

## Display & Behavior
- **Multi-display support** — overlays span all connected monitors independently
- **Night dimming** — subtly fades effects between 10pm and 5am
- **Seasonal auto-switch** — Snow in winter, Rain in spring, and so on
- **Low Power Mode** — caps animation to ~30 fps to save energy

## App
- **Global hotkey** — pause and resume from anywhere, fully customizable
- **Onboarding walkthrough** — pick your weather and intensity on first launch
- **Launch at Login** — start wthrr automatically when you log in
