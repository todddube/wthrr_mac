# wthrr 1.02b — What's New

## Aurora Birds
- **Animated birds in Aurora mode** — silhouette birds drift across the aurora sky, flapping organically with independent speeds, sizes, and phases; no two birds move in sync
- **Bird Count** — slider in Appearance settings (0 = off, up to 20); labeled Off / Few / Flock / Many / Murmur
- **Bird Size** — size multiplier from Tiny to Huge (0.5× – 3.0×); default wingspan ~28 pt
- **Wildness** — controls per-bird variation in size, speed, altitude, and flap rate (Uniform → Wild); at 0 you get a tidy flock, at 1 birds scatter chaotically across the sky

## Settings Weather Picker
- **Switch weather from within settings** — a persistent icon + label picker sits between the tab toolbar and content area on every settings tab; selecting a type updates the live overlay immediately and the tab controls refresh to match (Melt Rate for Snow, Bird controls for Aurora, Effect Density for AI Storm, etc.)
- Picker is disabled and dimmed when Live Weather is active

## Bug Fixes
- **Thunderstorm label truncation** — "Thunderstorm" now scales down gracefully in the menu bar popover header instead of clipping
